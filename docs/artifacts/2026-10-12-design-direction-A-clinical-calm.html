1. Direction and name

A reference that stays quiet, so the medicine, its evidence and its review status are the loudest things on screen.

2

Principles

Each one is a rule a designer can apply to a screen today, mapped to the Apple HIG or Material 3 idea it borrows.

1

Defer to the medicine

Chrome recedes into neutral surfaces. Brand names, strengths and codes carry the weight; controls appear at full strength only where a decision is made.

DeferenceTonal surfaces
2

Absence is never safety

"No data" has its own material: a grey hatch that can never be mistaken for green. Unknown severity is outlined and named, never quietly filed as low risk.

ClarityResponsibilityColour roles
3

Source beside result

The reviewer reads the DGDA register and the extraction on the same row, so each check is one horizontal glance rather than a memory exercise.

Grouping and mappingList-detail layout
4

One accent, earned

A single cobalt marks the next useful action and the current selection. Severity colours are semantic and never double as decoration.

HierarchyPrimary role, emphasis
5

Depth means structure

Only the toolbar, sidebar and decision bar are translucent. Content stays solid and flat, so material always reads as "frame", never as "data".

Depth, materialsElevation by tone
6

Every state says what to do next

Errors name what happened, what was not saved and the one action that fixes it. Motion is critically damped and answers a person's action, nothing else.

Feedback, agencyState layers, easing
7

Keyboard first, pointer welcome

A pharmacist working through 10 records should never reach for the mouse. Every decision has a key, and the key is printed on the button.

FlexibilityAdaptive

From the register to a record you can trust

Every brand travels the same four steps. The last one is a person.

  1. 1

    Read the DGDA register

    Trade name, company, dosage form and the generic text exactly as filed.

    "Paracetamol 500 mg"
  2. 2

    Extract the medicine

    Substances and strengths become a brand-independent clinical drug with international codes.

    Acetaminophen 500 mg tablet
  3. 3

    Run automated checks

    Four stage checks flag anything that loaded, parsed or matched badly.

    DGDA load, substances, clinical, DDInter
  4. 4

    Pharmacist approves

    A pharmacist compares source and result. Only then is the record public.

    Until then: "Not yet reviewed by a pharmacist"
