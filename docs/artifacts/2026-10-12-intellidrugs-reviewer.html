IntelliDrugs Reviewer Workspace

The desk where a pharmacist checks each record in batch 0001-pilot against the DGDA register before it goes public. Every record, check note, code, count and interaction pair below is the live pilot data.

  1. 1Click a record, or click anywhere in the workspace and move with J and K.
  2. 2Press A for Looks correct. You then have 8 seconds to press U before it saves. Enter never approves.
  3. 3Press R to report an issue, E for evidence, ? for every shortcut.
  4. 4Use the controls to switch role and to trigger each failure. Decisions stay in this page and are never sent anywhere.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Shortcuts work while focus is in the workspace. They pause while you type in a field, and ignore Ctrl, Cmd and Alt combinations.

    A
    Looks correct, then an 8-second undo window
    U
    Undo during the window
    R
    Report an issue
    JK

    Evidence: raw DGDA row and pipeline notes
    S
    Open the DGDA source

    Reviewer journey

    From the invite to an approved batch, in the order a pharmacist meets each screen. The record names and checks are from the pilot. Approvals, the email and the completed batch are examples.

      Reviewer roles come by invite only. The email names the role, the batch and the Google account to use.
