Direction and product name

Direction B

Material Precision

A tonal, component-led system that gives every state of a drug record its own recognisable form, so pharmacists review fast and prescribers never mistake missing data for safety.

mool মূল

Mool is Bengali for root or origin. It names what the product does: it takes any brand on the shelf down to the substance at its root. Short, the same in Bangla and English, and the l in the wordmark grows below the baseline as that root, in the seed colour.

suchi সূচি

Index or catalogue. The list you look things up in.

tolika তালিকা

List or register. Closest to the DGDA register itself.

Principles

Seven rules that decide every screen below. Each names the Apple HIG idea (outlined tag) and the Material 3 idea (tonal tag) it puts to work.

  1. 1

    Absence has a shape

    Missing interaction data gets a hatched, dashed, neutral surface and fixed wording. It is never green, never blank, and never collapsed into “no interactions”.

    ResponsibilityClarityCommunicate state
  2. 2

    One seed, every role

    All brand and surface colour comes from one prescription-ink blue. Red, amber, green and teal are reserved for meaning, so the brand never looks like a warning or an approval.

    Dynamic colourHarmonyCraft
  3. 3

    Surfaces carry hierarchy

    Depth comes from five tonal surface levels, not shadows. Only things that float over content (decision bar, snackbar, popover) cast one.

    Tonal elevationDeferenceDepth
  4. 4

    Source beside claim

    The reviewer reads what DGDA says and what the pipeline concluded on the same row, with the relation named: matches, transformed or added.

    Grouping and mappingList–detail layout
  5. 5

    Every press answers

    State layers at 8, 10 and 12 percent on hover, focus and press. Feedback starts on pointer-down, and keyboard focus is always visible.

    State layersFeedback
  6. 6

    Decide fast, undo safely

    Decisions live in a persistent bar with single-key shortcuts. Publishing waits five seconds behind an Undo, so a slip costs nothing and nothing needs a confirm dialog.

    AgencyForgivenessSnackbar
  7. 7

    Dense, still legible

    Compact density for laptops, Roboto Flex optical sizing, tabular numerals and a mono face only for codes, so XM86W0 and B01AA03 can be compared character by character.

    DensityType scaleTypography

How a record becomes public

Nothing appears on Mool until a pharmacist has compared it with the register.

  1. 1
    Loaded from DGDA

    Brand, company, dosage form and content come straight from the register.

  2. 2
    Substances extracted

    Ingredients and strengths are parsed. Salts are removed, so Warfarin Sodium becomes Warfarin.

  3. 3
    Codes and interactions matched

    ICD-11, RxNorm, ATC, DrugBank, SNOMED CT, and the DDInter 2.0 entry for the right route.

  4. 4
    Four automated checks

    Each stage reports ok, warning or failure, so reviewers look at the uncertain parts first.

  5. 5
    A pharmacist approves

    Only a pharmacist's approval publishes. Everything else stays marked “Not yet reviewed by a pharmacist”.

If a record is re-run after approval, it goes back to review before it changes in public.

Why it looks like this

  • A banner, not a dialog. It sits where the eye already is and keeps the old data visible but dimmed, so the reviewer sees what they were looking at.
  • Buttons are disabled with the reason written next to them, not silently. Nothing the reviewer typed is lost.
  • “See what changed” opens the same source and extraction table with changed cells marked, so a re-run after approval can be re-approved in seconds.
  • Warning colour, not critical. Nothing is wrong yet; the data simply moved.

WhyIt is what people buy and doctors write. The register is the source of truth for it.

WhyTwo brands with the same substance, strength and form are the same drug for prescribing.

WhyBangladesh says paracetamol, international codes say acetaminophen. One substance holds both names.

WhyCodes let EMRs, pharmacies and other databases talk about the same thing.

WhyInteractions belong to substances, so every paracetamol brand inherits them.
