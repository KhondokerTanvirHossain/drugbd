Name and wordmark

The product is IntelliDrugs, served at drugdb.eliooo.org. Three typographic wordmarks, all set in Onest from Direction A. Option A is recommended and used in this board's header.

Principles

Direction A's seven, tightened after review. Each is a rule you can apply to a screen today, mapped to the Apple HIG idea it follows and the Material 3 structure that carries it.

  1. 1

    Defer to the medicine

    Chrome recedes into neutral surfaces. Brand names, strengths, ids and codes carry the weight. Controls appear at full strength only where a decision is made.

    HIGDeferenceM3Tonal surface roles
  2. 2

    Absence is never safety

    No data has its own material, the hatch, and its own fixed sentence. Unknown severity is named "Severity not classified by DDInter" and is never merged with Minor. Pairs that involve a no-data substance are "not checked", never counted as checked.

    HIGResponsibility, clarityM3Colour roles, outline variant
  3. 3

    Source beside result

    The reviewer reads the DGDA register and the extraction on the same table row, with a worded verdict between them: Matches, Normalised, Salt removed, Added or Differs.

    HIGGrouping and mappingM3List-detail canonical layout
  4. 4

    One accent, one meaning per mark

    Cobalt marks selection, the next action and focus. Every other mark means exactly one thing: square chips are severity, rounded pills are review status, green is pharmacist approval, the hatch is no data.

    HIGConsistency, hierarchyM3Primary role, component states
  5. 5

    Depth means structure

    Only the toolbar, sidebar and decision bar are translucent. Content is solid and flat, so material always reads as frame and never as data.

    HIGMaterials, depthM3Elevation by tone
  6. 6

    Every decision can be taken back

    "Looks correct" waits 8 seconds before it saves, with Undo on screen and on the U key. A re-run after approval returns the record to review, and a stale page says so before anything is saved.

    HIGAgency, forgivenessM3Snackbar with action
  7. 7

    Keyboard first, printed on the button

    A pharmacist working through a batch never needs the mouse. Every decision has a letter key printed on its button. Enter never approves, and shortcuts stop while you type.

    HIGFlexibility, feedbackM3Focus state layer, adaptive

Motion

Critically damped, Material 3 easing curves. Nothing moves unless a person caused it. The one long animation is the 8-second commit countdown, and it is linear because it is a clock.

Spring equivalent for panes and sheets: damping 1.0, response 0.35 s. Reduced motion turns every move into a 1 ms change, stops the spinner and shows the countdown as a full bar with the words "Saves in 8 seconds".

Every state has one shape, one icon and one wording, and no mark is reused for another meaning. Shape tells you the family: square is severity, rounded is review status, card is a stage check, table cell is a match verdict.

Components

Every component in every state. Forced states use the documentation classes .is-hover, .is-focus and .is-pressed; in a product they come from the real pointer and keyboard. Data shown is the live 0001-pilot batch unless it carries an Example marker.

Buttons .btn--primary .btn--tonal .btn--plain .btn--destructive

EnabledHoverFocusPressedDisabledLoading Primary Tonal Plain Destructive

State layers follow Material 3: 8% hover, 10% focus, 12% pressed, drawn in the label colour. Press also scales to 0.97 on pointer-down. Destructive is for actions that throw work away; "Report issue" is not destructive. A loading button keeps its width and says what it is doing.

    Do and don't

    The mistakes the vocabulary exists to prevent, each next to the correct form.

    Don't: hatch for an unreviewed record

    Not yet reviewed

    The hatch means no interaction data. Using it for review status makes "unreviewed" and "no data" look the same.

    Do: the clock pill

    Not yet reviewed by a pharmacist

    Rounded pill, hollow clock, exact wording.

    Don't: green for a substance with no data

    No interactions

    Domperidone has no DDInter entry. Green and "No interactions" read as safe.

    Do: the no-data statement

    No interaction data in DDInter for this substance. This does not mean it is safe to combine.

    Don't: fold unknown severity into Minor

    Minor 369

    Warfarin has 71 Minor and 298 unclassified pairs. Adding them hides 298 pairs DDInter never graded.

    Do: keep them apart

    Minor 71Severity not classified by DDInter 298

    Don't: Enter to approve

    Enter is the key people press to confirm anything. One stray press would publish a record.

    Do: a letter key and an undo window

    A deliberate key, then 8 seconds to press U.

    Don't: rounded severity, or an icon alone

    Major

    A rounded red chip reads as a review status. A warning icon with no word cannot be told from the Moderate tint at a glance.

    Do: square chip with glyph, icon with word

    MajorWarning

    Warfarin + Aspirin: bleeding risk, avoid or monitor INR.
